RESTORE — Prayer Warrior Journal & Daily Battle Plan, 2027 edition

Coming to Amazon · 2027 edition

The book our men carry is about to be a book you can carry.

“This journal is not a casual devotional. It is a daily operational logbook.”

RESTORE was written for the men of Joseph’s Home — a year of daily pages a man works from before sunrise to lights-out. The 2027 edition is coming to Amazon in paperback and for Kindle, so families, prayer partners, churches and small groups can walk the same year alongside the man they are praying for.

  • A page for every day, in six fixed sections: Scripture Anchor, Tactical Prayer Directive, the Watch List, Orders of the Day, Holy Spirit Intel, and the Evening Debrief.
  • A weekly two-page reset — Sabbath Debrief and Strategic Deployment — and a milestone debrief at the close of each month.
  • Twelve monthly themes, beginning with Chosen People.
  • The Tactical Scripture Index: what to turn to during craving, shame, rage, isolation, grief, fear, exhaustion, or financial panic — with a practical next action for each.
  • The RESTORE Covenant, signed in front of an accountability brother or mentor.

Prayer partner bookmark

Ribbon-weight card that keeps a man’s place at the Scripture Index, and gives his prayer partner the same week’s ground to pray over.

Fridge magnet

The same week on the refrigerator door at home, where the family that is praying for him will actually see it.

The journal, the bookmark and the magnet are now part of the discipleship program: every man in the house gets a copy, and every prayer partner gets the matching set.
