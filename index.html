If you are in trouble tonight

You do not have to wait for office hours.

If someone is in immediate danger, call 911. For free, confidential help at any hour of the day or night, call or text 988. For treatment referrals near you, SAMHSA’s national helpline is 1-800-662-4357. None of those numbers will judge you, and none of them cost anything.

The next step

If you are ready, the door is open.

The application asks a great deal of you, and it is meant to. Take it slowly, tell the truth, and save it as you go. If you get stuck, call and leave a message and someone will call you back.
