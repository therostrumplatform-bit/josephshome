Scripture for the hard nights

Keep this page. Bookmark it on your phone. It is arranged by what is happening in you, not by book and chapter.

Joseph’s Home · Gulfport, Mississippi · 2 min read · updated August 2026

“Thy word have I hid in mine heart, that I might not sin against thee.”

Psalm 119:11 · KJV

When you want to use

There hath no temptation taken you but such as is common to man: but God is faithful, who will not suffer you to be tempted above that ye are able; but will with the temptation also make a way to escape. — 1 Corinthians 10:13

Submit yourselves therefore to God. Resist the devil, and he will flee from you. — James 4:7

When you are ashamed

There is therefore now no condemnation to them which are in Christ Jesus. — Romans 8:1

As far as the east is from the west, so far hath he removed our transgressions from us. — Psalm 103:12

When you have fallen again

It is of the Lord’s mercies that we are not consumed, because his compassions fail not. They are new every morning: great is thy faithfulness. — Lamentations 3:22-23

For a just man falleth seven times, and riseth up again. — Proverbs 24:16

When you are afraid

Fear thou not; for I am with thee: be not dismayed; for I am thy God: I will strengthen thee; yea, I will help thee. — Isaiah 41:10

Be still, and know that I am God. — Psalm 46:10

When you cannot sleep

I laid me down and slept; I awaked; for the Lord sustained me. — Psalm 3:5

Come unto me, all ye that labour and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest. — Matthew 11:28

When nobody understands

The Lord is nigh unto them that are of a broken heart; and saveth such as be of a contrite spirit. — Psalm 34:18

A friend loveth at all times, and a brother is born for adversity. — Proverbs 17:17

When you want to quit the program

And let us not be weary in well doing: for in due season we shall reap, if we faint not. — Galatians 6:9

Being confident of this very thing, that he which hath begun a good work in you will perform it until the day of Jesus Christ. — Philippians 1:6

When you start to think you are fine now

Wherefore let him that thinketh he standeth take heed lest he fall. — 1 Corinthians 10:12

Pride goeth before destruction, and an haughty spirit before a fall. — Proverbs 16:18

Reading scripture is not a substitute for telling a human being. Read the verse, then pick up the phone. If it is an emergency, call 911; for free confidential help any hour, call or text 988.
