IF TONIGHT IS THE EMERGENCY

Do not wait on us.

If someone is in immediate danger, call 911. For free, confidential help at any hour, call or text 988. For treatment referrals near you, SAMHSA’s national helpline is 1-800-662-4357. Then write to us in the morning.
